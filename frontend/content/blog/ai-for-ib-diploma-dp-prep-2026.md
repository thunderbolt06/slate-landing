The International Baccalaureate Diploma Programme is the hardest two year curriculum in the world. The global mean score in May 2025 was 30.32 out of 45. Roughly 79% of candidates earned the diploma. Hitting 40+ puts you in the top 11% of all DP candidates worldwide and gets you a serious look from Oxford, Cambridge, top US schools, and the rest of the global league.

This guide is for DP1 and DP2 students who want a real score, not a participation trophy. AI tutoring in 2026 is built for the IB workload because the IB workload is exactly what breaks human tutoring.

## Why the DP Breaks Most Tutoring Models

The DP asks you to do six subjects at HL or SL level, write a 4,000 word Extended Essay, take TOK, and log CAS. A human tutor can cover one subject. Maybe two. The DP is six. You cannot afford six tutors and the schedule does not fit them anyway.

AI tutoring fits because:

1. You can ask one tool about Maths AA HL, then Biology HL, then English A: Lang Lit HL, in a single afternoon.
2. The AI does not have a calendar conflict at 9 pm when your Physics IA is due Friday.
3. Each subject can have its own context with its own past papers, IB syllabus, and rubric.

What you give up is the deep relationship a single human tutor builds with you. What you get back is coverage and availability.

## The Internal Assessment Problem

Every DP subject has an Internal Assessment worth 20% to 30% of the final grade. Across six subjects, IAs are about 25% of your total diploma score on average.

IAs are where students lose the most points to time mismanagement. The Physics IA, the Math IA, the History IA, the English IO, and the Chemistry IA all cluster around DP2 January. Add the Extended Essay deadline and the TOK essay, and you have a six week window where everything is due.

AI tutoring helps with each IA in three specific ways:

1. Rubric breakdown. The AI can take the official subject guide and translate the criteria into checkable items. You no longer guess what Criterion B in the Math IA actually means.
2. Outline review. Paste your draft outline. The AI scores it against the criteria and flags where the argument is too thin to hit the top band.
3. Feedback rounds. Most students get one or two rounds of feedback from their teacher. With AI, you can do five rounds before your teacher sees the draft, so the version they read is already strong.

The trap to avoid: do not have the AI write the IA. Examiners are trained on AI prose now, and the IB has tightened academic honesty rules in 2025. Use AI for diagnosis and structure, not generation.

## Subject by Subject Strategy

### Maths AA HL

Maths AA HL is the most failed HL subject. The 2025 grade distribution showed roughly 13% of students earned a 7, but 31% earned a 4 or below. The problem is volume. The syllabus covers calculus, vectors, complex numbers, statistics, and proof.

AI tutoring strategy:

- Use past papers from 2014 onwards. The new syllabus started in 2021 but the older question types still test the same skills.
- Work paper 1 in 90 minute blocks with no calculator. Paper 2 with the GDC. Paper 3 the AA HL only investigation question.
- Have the AI grade your method marks. Method marks decide HL scores more than final answers.

### Biology HL

Biology HL is heavy on content and the IA design is harder than students expect. The 2025 mean was 4.18 at HL.

AI tutoring strategy:

- Use the AI to convert the syllabus understandings into Anki style cards. Roughly 1,500 cards covers the HL syllabus.
- For Paper 2 essays, draft outlines and have the AI score against the markschemes.
- For the IA, the AI is excellent at flagging when your variable manipulation is not specific enough to hit the top band of Personal Engagement and Exploration.

### English A: Language and Literature HL

Most students underestimate English HL until the Paper 1 unseen text destroys them in mocks. The AI is more useful here than in any other subject because it can take any text and run a structured analysis with you.

The drill: feed the AI a non literary text, ask it to play examiner, and have it score your Paper 1 response against the criteria. After 20 of these, your Paper 1 marks will move two grade bands.

### History HL

History HL Paper 3 is the hardest paper in the diploma. Regional in depth questions reward students who know specific evidence dates and figures and can synthesize across topics.

AI tutoring strategy:

- Build a 100 fact list per topic, dates, names, treaties, ratios.
- Use the AI to generate Paper 3 style questions and grade your responses against the markscheme.
- The AI is better at flagging when your essay is descriptive rather than analytical. That single shift is the difference between a 5 and a 7.

### Chemistry HL

The 2023 syllabus changes shifted Chemistry HL more toward conceptual understanding and away from rote memorization. The data based question on Paper 2 is the biggest mark differentiator.

AI tutoring strategy:

- Use the AI to walk you through enthalpy, kinetics, and organic mechanisms with worked examples.
- For Paper 2 data questions, the AI can pull example data sets and walk you through interpretation drills.

### Physics HL

Physics HL is content heavy and the math is harder than students expect. The Paper 3 option choice matters less than the core preparation.

AI tutoring strategy:

- Use the AI as a derivation buddy. Most students memorize equations without understanding them, which fails in transfer questions.
- For the IA, the AI is the best tool available for refining your independent variable choice. Most students pick a topic that cannot reach the top band of Personal Engagement.

## TOK and Extended Essay

TOK and EE are the two pieces that move the matrix grade by up to 3 points. Most students treat them as afterthoughts. That is a mistake.

For TOK, AI tutoring helps you:

1. Map the IB prescribed titles against the markscheme criteria.
2. Distinguish first order claims from second order claims, which is the dividing line between a B and an A.
3. Stress test your knowledge questions for whether they are answerable in 1,600 words.

For the EE, the AI is invaluable for:

1. Narrowing your research question. Most failed EEs fail at the question stage.
2. Building a literature base. The AI cannot give you sources you can cite, but it can suggest where to look.
3. Structural review. The IB markscheme is precise about argument structure. The AI checks structure faster than your supervisor will.

## Workload Survival

DP students burn out for one reason: the workload spikes are not distributed evenly. November of DP1, January of DP2, and April of DP2 are the killer months. The rest of the time you can breathe.

AI tutoring helps with workload distribution:

- Plan IA work to start three months before the deadline, not three weeks.
- Use the AI to draft outlines during the calm months so you only do refinement during the spike months.
- Set up a weekly review session where you feed the AI your upcoming deadlines and ask for a prioritized task list.

The students who hit 40+ in May 2025 mostly did this without the AI. With AI in 2026, the same plan is achievable with less burnout.
